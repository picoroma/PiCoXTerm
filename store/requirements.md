## Only you can do these (account, money, identity)

1. **Microsoft Partner Center developer account.** One-time fee (currently a one-off individual
   registration charge, historically in the ~$19 USD range, waived for some .edu/MVP cases — worth
   checking the current price in Partner Center before paying) plus whatever tax ID / company
   details Microsoft asks for in Italy.
2. **Reserve the app name** ("PiCoXterm") in Partner Center. This is what creates the **Publisher
   CN** and **Package Family Name** the `Package.appxmanifest` needs — they don't exist before this
   step, so I can't template the manifest's identity block yet.
3. **IARC age-rating questionnaire**, filled in Partner Center under your account.
4. **Support contact** Microsoft will display (email or page) and a **public URL for the privacy
   policy** (`store/privacy-policy.md` is ready to publish — GitHub Pages, or just a raw link to
   this file in the repo, both work for Microsoft).
5. **Original hero image and tagline** — you've said you're preparing these yourself.
6. First submission review and any back-and-forth with Microsoft's certification team.
